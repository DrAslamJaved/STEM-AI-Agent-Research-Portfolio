# Phase 5 Controlled Drafting Protocol

Drafting is template-based and requires finalizable claim identifiers from the
Phase 4 ledger. A claim must be human-approved or experimentally supported and
have verified supporting evidence. Results sections additionally require a
saved mapping of analysis values.

The module produces a traceable draft artifact containing the referenced claims,
analysis keys, and style profile. It does not call a generative model, retrieve
sources, invent results, or declare a manuscript final.
