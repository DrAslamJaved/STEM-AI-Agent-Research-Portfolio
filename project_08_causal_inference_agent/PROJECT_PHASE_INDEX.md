# Project 08 phase index

1. Foundation and mathematical contract
2. Synthetic structural causal models with known effects
3. Causal-question parsing and prediction-versus-causation classification
4. Causal DAG construction and assumption audit
5. Estimand identification and non-identifiability rejection
6. Baseline causal-effect estimation
7. Cross-fitted AIPW and heterogeneous-effect estimation
8. Overlap, balance, propensity, and weight diagnostics
9. Refutation and stress testing
10. Hidden-confounding sensitivity analysis and robustness bounds
11. IHDP and Lalonde benchmark evaluation with ATE error, PEHE, and interval coverage
12. End-to-end orchestration, qualified reporting, CI, and release closure

Every phase is cumulative. A later phase may not bypass an earlier safety gate.
