# Literature Search and Reference-Verification Strategy

## Status

Phase 02. No literature search has yet been executed. No candidate similarity formula is approved.

## Search themes

- fuzzy similarity measures
- similarity between fuzzy sets
- cardinality-based fuzzy similarity
- fuzzy Jaccard, Dice, Cosine, and Tversky measures
- sigma-count fuzzy cardinality
- fuzzy similarity axioms, boundedness, reflexivity, symmetry, identity, and monotonicity
- fuzzy similarity in clustering and classification

## Discovery sources

Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, Crossref, Scopus, Web of Science, MathSciNet, zbMATH, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, and publisher databases.

## Verification procedure

For every retained source, verify authors, title, venue, year, DOI or stable URL, and the exact page, equation, definition, theorem, or proposition supporting each claim.

## Inclusion criteria

Retain primary research or authoritative sources that define fuzzy similarity measures, analyze their axioms, establish counterexamples, or report reproducible applications.

## Exclusion criteria

Exclude unverifiable citations, duplicate records, unsupported AI-generated references, and sources unrelated to fuzzy-set similarity.

## Search records

Future searches will be recorded in `literature/search_log.csv` with query, source, date, result count, retained references, and notes.

Future claims will be recorded in `literature/reference_verification_matrix.csv` with claim, source, exact location, verification status, and reviewer decision.

## Formula gate

No candidate formula may be proposed until the search and reference-verification records are reviewed by the human researcher.

All current literature claims remain `TO BE EXECUTED/VERIFIED`.
