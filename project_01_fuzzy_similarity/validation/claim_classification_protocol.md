# Claim Classification Protocol

## Purpose

This protocol prevents unsupported mathematical, empirical, or literature claims from entering Project 1 reports, code documentation, or conclusions.

No candidate fuzzy similarity measure is approved at this stage.

## Claim statuses

| Status | Meaning | Minimum evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| `ESTABLISHED` | Existing theory verified from primary literature | Verified source and exact supporting location |
| `AGENT_PROPOSED` | Candidate claim, formula, proof idea, or interpretation generated by an AI agent | Agent trace and explicit proposal label |
| `HUMAN_APPROVED` | Candidate formulation accepted for analysis | Recorded human decision and stated assumptions |
| `PROOF_DRAFTED` | A proof has been written but not accepted | Complete proof draft and review record |
| `PROVED_VERIFIED` | A result has been formally established | Human-reviewed proof and independent verification |
| `EXPERIMENTALLY_SUPPORTED` | Numerical or empirical evidence supports a claim | Reproducible experiment and preserved outputs |
| `REFUTED` | Claim is false under its stated assumptions | Valid counterexample or contradiction |
| `INCONCLUSIVE` | Available evidence does not justify a conclusion | Record of unresolved issue and next action |

## Mandatory distinction

The following implications are forbidden:

\[
\text{EXPERIMENTALLY\_SUPPORTED} \not\Rightarrow \text{PROVED\_VERIFIED}
\]

\[
S(A,A)=1 \not\Rightarrow [S(A,B)=1 \Leftrightarrow A=B].
\]

A numerical test, symbolic calculation, or repeated random simulation may reveal a defect or support a conjecture, but cannot by itself establish a theorem.

## Claim ledger fields

Every substantive claim must record:

- claim identifier;
- exact claim statement;
- status;
- assumptions and domain restrictions;
- origin: literature, agent, human, proof, code, or experiment;
- verified reference or agent-trace location;
- proof, test, or counterexample artifact;
- human decision;
- UTC decision timestamp;
- limitations and next action.

## Decision rules

1. A literature-derived statement is not `ESTABLISHED` until its source and mathematical relevance are verified.
2. An AI-generated formula remains `AGENT_PROPOSED` until human approval.
3. A proof remains `PROOF_DRAFTED` until its assumptions and all logical steps are independently reviewed.
4. A counterexample must satisfy the definition’s stated domain before a claim is marked `REFUTED`.
5. Numerical outputs may receive only `EXPERIMENTALLY_SUPPORTED` status.
6. Any ambiguity about domain, denominator-zero conventions, identity, or monotonicity requires `INCONCLUSIVE` status until resolved.
7. A claim may be promoted only by adding evidence; it may never be promoted merely because an agent expresses confidence.

## Required human interventions

Human review is mandatory when an agent:

- asserts novelty without verified literature;
- omits a domain assumption;
- treats a numerical result as proof;
- infers identity from reflexivity;
- makes an undefined denominator convention silently;
- generalizes a result beyond its stated parameter range.

## Audit rule

Every final conclusion must trace to one or more ledger entries and preserve its highest justified status. Unsupported claims must be removed, narrowed, or labelled `INCONCLUSIVE`.
