## Final claim boundary

Project 09 demonstrates an auditable retrospective materials-ML workflow. It
does not claim prospective laboratory discovery, label savings at the locked
Phase 03 threshold, or superiority of the fixed Phase 08 graph model. Any
future positive graph-assisted discovery claim requires a newly preregistered
model-selection and acquisition protocol.
